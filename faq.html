Designed to be understood.

Straight answers about what AI Workspace is, how it works, and what it isn’t.

01

General

  • An original product concept: one workspace for AI chat, documents, writing, reports, knowledge, and projects, designed and built as a portfolio project by Handsome.

  • AI Workspace is presented as an interactive product demo. Its model names and generated responses are illustrative, so the experience can focus on clear AI product design rather than provider claims.

  • The sign-in and sign-up screens are a demo flow. They show how the experience would feel, and no real account is created.

  • Individual knowledge workers, founders, creatives, and teams who rely on research, writing, and shared context to get work done.

02

The product

03

Teams & security
