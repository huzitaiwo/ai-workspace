Design principles

Six ideas that shape every screen.

They guide decisions from the smallest component to the largest workflow.

  • Clarity over cleverness

    Hierarchy and plain language carry the intelligence, not decoration.

  • Context stays visible

    Every answer shows the project, sources, and instructions behind it.

  • Calm motion

    Movement explains what changed, then gets out of the way. Reduced motion is respected.

  • Every state is designed

    Empty, loading, error, and success states say what happened and what to do next.

  • Accessible by default

    Keyboard paths, focus handling, and screen-reader labels ship with each component.

  • Honest about AI

    Models are illustrative, responses are labeled, and generated work carries a disclosure.

AI states

What happens, why, and what you can do.

Every AI operation answers three questions, so the interface never leaves you guessing.

The states of an AI operation and what each means for the user
State What is happening What you can do
Idle Ready for a prompt. Type, attach files, or choose context.
Processing Your request was received. Wait a moment or cancel.
Thinking The model is working through the task. Stop generation at any time.
Streaming The answer is arriving. Stop, or keep reading as it appears.
Complete The answer is finished. Copy, edit, regenerate, or rate it.
Cancelled You stopped the response. Continue it or start again.
Error The response could not be generated. Retry, or edit the prompt.
Rate limited Too many requests in a short time. Wait for the countdown, then retry.
Context limit The conversation is too long for the model. Start a new thread or remove sources.
Service unavailable The AI service is not responding. Try again shortly. Your draft is kept.
